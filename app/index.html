Welcome to CC Planner

CC Planner helps you know what protective equipment you have, what condition it is in, what needs replacing, and what to bring to a particular situation.

The core loop: track your inventory, plan an activity, compare the recommendations with what you own, and replenish what is missing.

How do you want to start?

You can change your mind later. Nothing is created without your say-so.

Your data stays here

Everything you enter is stored locally on this device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Local storage does not by itself provide encryption. You can optionally encrypt your data with a password. If you forget the password, the data cannot be recovered.
